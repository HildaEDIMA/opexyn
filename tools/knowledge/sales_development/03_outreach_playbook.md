# Outreach Playbook

## Default cadence (adapt to client's rules/autonomy settings)

A single lead, once qualified, follows this sequence unless they reply or
disqualify earlier:

1. **Day 0** — Initial email, highly personalized (reference something
   specific about them/their company).
2. **Day 3** — LinkedIn connection request with a short, non-salesy note
   (if LinkedIn is a connected tool).
3. **Day 6** — Follow-up email #1 — add a new angle or piece of value
   (e.g. a relevant insight, not just "just following up").
4. **Day 12** — Follow-up email #2 — short, low-pressure, offers an easy out
   ("If now's not the right time, no worries — mind if I check back in a
   quarter?").
5. **Day 20** — Breakup email — final, polite, leaves the door open.

Stop the sequence immediately at any reply, positive or negative, and switch
to responsive mode (see objection handling doc).

## Email structure that works

- **Subject line**: short, specific, no clickbait. Reference their company,
  role, or a real trigger event. Avoid ALL CAPS, exclamation points, "Quick
  question" clichés.
- **Opening line**: personalized, proves you did homework. Never start with
  "My name is X and I work at Y."
- **Body**: 2-4 short sentences. State the problem you think they have and
  how the client's product addresses it. No feature dumps.
- **Proof point**: one concrete detail (only if backed by the client's
  actual knowledge base/briefing — never invented).
- **Call to action**: one clear, low-friction ask. Prefer "Worth a 15-min
  call?" over "Let me know if you'd like to schedule a comprehensive demo at
  your convenience."
- **Signature**: matches the client's configured sender identity.

## LinkedIn outreach notes

- Connection note: 1-2 sentences max, no pitch. Earn the connection first.
- After connecting, wait at least a day before sending a message.
- Never send a generic templated LinkedIn pitch — it reads as spam
  immediately on that channel more than email.

## Volume and pacing

- Respect the daily outreach quota configured for the client's plan.
- Spread sends across the day rather than batching all at once — looks more
  human and avoids spam filters.
- Never send more than one cold touch to the same person on the same day
  across channels.

## What to log after every touch

- Channel used, timestamp, message sent (or reference to template + fields).
- Any reply received, sentiment, and next action taken.
- Updated lead stage (New → Contacted → Engaged → Meeting Booked →
  Disqualified).
